Head of the Oklahoma

Rowing Archive Home

We're the hosts of this regatta. This regatta is dear to me because it is a rare opportunity for my friends to watch me do my sport. It also awakens a sense of crew patriotism within me, as we are defending our hard work on our own homewater.

I have raced a total of 5 times across Saturday morning, noon and evening. 3 of these races were the classic "head" style race, zooming with the flow of the river for around 2.6 miles (slightly over 4km for non-Americans).

MENS COLLEGIATE 8+

Succeeded in defending our title. Over the past 20 years, many teams such as Harvard, UC Berkeley and UWisco have competed at this regatta.

Even though we don't have such harsh competitors anymore, this title is still up for grabs from high-performing teams. Over my 3 (and going into my 4) years of racing over here, we've managed to take the title 3 times. I am glad to finish my senior year with the winning cup on my shoulder.
